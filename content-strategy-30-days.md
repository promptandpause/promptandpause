# Prompt & Pause — 30-Day Content Launch Strategy

> **Goal**: Drive traffic, build community, generate sign-ups  
> **Live site**: [promptandpause.com](https://www.promptandpause.com)  
> **Brand voice**: Calm, honest, empowering — never preachy. Speak like a thoughtful friend, not a therapist.  
> **Color palette**: Purple/violet tones, warm neutrals  
> **Tagline**: *Transform Your Mental Wellness Journey with Daily Reflection*

---

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Role | Tone | Post Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Community hub — carousels, quote cards, Reels | Warm, reflective, inviting | 1 post/day + 3-4 Stories/day |
| **TikTok** | Discovery & virality — short-form video | Raw, relatable, conversational | 1 video/day |
| **Twitter/X** | Thought leadership & daily prompts | Concise, insightful, thread-friendly | 2-3 tweets/day |
| **Facebook** | Intentional reach & paid ads | Professional, benefit-driven | 1 post/day + ads |

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Prompt of the Day** — Share an actual reflection prompt (your core product in action)
2. **Pause Moments** — Calming, aesthetic content that makes people stop scrolling
3. **Real Talk** — Relatable mental wellness truths (not toxic positivity)
4. **Behind the Build** — Founder story, why you built this, transparency
5. **Social Proof & Testimonials** — User wins, before/after mindset shifts
6. **Education** — Why reflection works, science-backed benefits

## TWITTER/X — 30-Day Strategy

### Role: Thought leadership, daily prompts, mental wellness insights

**Daily rhythm:**
- **Morning (8-9am)**: Daily prompt tweet — share the actual prompt of the day
- **Midday (12-1pm)**: Insight/thread/engagement tweet
- **Evening (optional)**: Reply to conversations, retweet relevant mental health content

### Content Types

#### 1. Daily Prompt Tweets (Every day)
Simple, clean format:

```
Today's prompt:

"What would you do differently if no one was watching?"

Sit with it. No rush.

#PromptAndPause #DailyReflection
```

#### 2. Threads (2x per week)
- **Thread ideas:**
  - "7 questions that will change how you see yourself (thread)"
  - "Why most people fail at journaling — and what to do instead"
  - "The difference between thinking and reflecting"
  - "I built a mental wellness app. Here's what I learned about people."
  - "5 focus areas that transform your reflection practice"
  - "What your mood patterns are trying to tell you"
  - "The science of self-awareness in 5 tweets"
  - "Why I left [previous career/situation] to build Prompt & Pause"

#### 3. Engagement Tweets (3x per week)
- "What's one question you wish someone would ask you?"
- "Unpopular opinion: You don't need a morning routine. You need a morning question."
- "Your mental health check-in for today: How are you, really? Reply with an emoji."
- "Hot take: Most self-help advice fails because it tells you what to think, not how to reflect."
- "Name one thing you're proud of this week. I'll go first."

#### 4. Product/Launch Tweets (2x per week)
- "We just launched Prompt & Pause — daily reflection prompts powered by AI. Free to start. [link]"

#### 5. Founder/Behind-the-Scenes (1x per week)
- "Week 2 of running Prompt & Pause. Here's what I've learned..."
- "Building in public: Our sign-up numbers, what's working, what's not"
- Share development milestones, user feedback, honest reflections

## FACEBOOK — 30-Day Strategy

### Role: Intentional reach, community building, paid ads

### Organic Posts (1/day)

Facebook favors longer-form, conversational posts. Repurpose Instagram content but **adapt the format**:

#### Content Mix:
- **40% Value posts** — Tips, benefits of reflection, "did you know" facts
- **30% Story posts** — Founder journey, user stories, behind-the-scenes
- **20% Product posts** — Features, how-to, sign-up CTAs
- **10% Community posts** — Questions, polls, discussions

#### Sample Posts:

**Value Post:**
> Most people spend 6+ hours on their phone daily but can't find 5 minutes to check in with themselves.
>
> That's not a time problem. That's a priority problem.
>
> Prompt & Pause sends you one question a day. Just one. It takes 5 minutes to answer. And over time, those 5 minutes compound into real self-awareness.
>
> Try it free → [link]

**Story Post:**
> I built Prompt & Pause because I was struggling.
>
> Not in a dramatic, rock-bottom way. In a quiet, "I don't know what I'm feeling" way. I'd go through entire weeks on autopilot. Busy but not present. Productive but not fulfilled.
>
> A therapist once told me: "You don't need more answers. You need better questions."
>
> That stuck with me. And eventually, it became this app.
>
> If you've ever felt like you're moving fast but going nowhere — this is for you.

**Community Post:**
> Quick check-in for everyone here:
>
> What's ONE word that describes how you're feeling right now?
>
> No judgment. No fixing. Just honesty.
>
> I'll go first: "hopeful" 💜

### Facebook Ads Strategy

#### Ad Types to Run:

**1. Awareness Ad (Week 1-2) — Budget: £5-10/day**
- **Format**: Video (repurpose your best-performing Reel/TikTok)
- **Audience**: Interest-based — mental health, self-improvement, journaling, meditation, mindfulness, therapy
- **Age**: 22-45
- **Geo**: UK + US
- **CTA**: "Learn More" → Landing page
- **Copy**: "Your daily reflection practice — in 5 minutes. Personalized prompts. Mood tracking. Zero pressure. Start free."

**2. Social Proof Ad (Week 2-3) — Budget: £5-10/day**
- **Format**: Carousel of testimonials or quote cards
- **Audience**: Retarget website visitors + lookalike of sign-ups
- **CTA**: "Sign Up" → Sign-up page
- **Copy**: "Join thousands building a daily reflection habit. Here's what they're saying..."

**3. Conversion Ad (Week 3-4) — Budget: £10-15/day**
- **Format**: Static image or short video showing the dashboard
- **Audience**: Retarget people who visited but didn't sign up
- **CTA**: "Start Free Trial"
- **Copy**: "You visited. You were curious. Your first prompt is waiting. No credit card needed."

**4. Premium Upsell Ad (Week 4) — Budget: £5/day**
- **Format**: Feature comparison graphic (Free vs Premium)
- **Audience**: Retarget existing free users
- **CTA**: "Upgrade Now"
- **Copy**: "Ready to go deeper? Unlock AI insights, custom focus areas, voice prompts, and more. From £4.99/mo."

#### Targeting Suggestions:
- **Interests**: Journaling, mindfulness, meditation, self-care, personal development, therapy, mental health awareness, Headspace, Calm, BetterHelp
- **Behaviors**: Engaged shoppers, mobile-first users
- **Lookalikes**: 1% lookalike of sign-ups once you have 100+

## Quote Card Design Specs (Instagram/TikTok Static Posts)

To match your brand:

- **Background**: Clean white (#FFFFFF) or warm cream (#F5F3EE) — matches your app's light mode
- **Text color**: Dark charcoal (#3D3D3D) for the quote
- **Accent**: Purple (#7E6BA5 or #C4B5E0) for highlights or the attribution line
- **Font**: Clean sans-serif (Inter, DM Sans, or similar)
- **Layout**: Centered text, generous whitespace, logo watermark bottom-center
- **Size**: 1080x1080 (square) for feed, 1080x1920 for Stories/Reels cover
